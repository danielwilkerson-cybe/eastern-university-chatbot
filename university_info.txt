--------------------------------------------------

APPLICATION PROCESS:

1. Choose the graduate program you want to study.
2. Review the admission requirements for that specific program.
3. Complete and submit the online application.
4. Submit the required transcripts and supporting documents.
5. Complete any additional requirements requested by the program.
6. Contact the admissions office if you need help with your application.

GENERAL ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS:

According to Eastern University's graduate catalog, general graduate admission criteria include:

- A bachelor's degree from an institutionally accredited college or university.
- A minimum undergraduate GPA of 2.5.
- Some programs may require a higher GPA.
- Some programs may require admission tests, interviews, essays, recommendation letters, or other documents.

IMPORTANT:
Admission requirements vary by graduate program. Applicants should always check the specific program requirements.

Graduate Financial Aid

Eastern University offers several types of financial assistance to eligible graduate students.

Available financial assistance may include:

Graduate fellowships

Graduate scholarships

Graduate assistantships

Federal Direct Unsubsidized Loans

Eastern Campus Employment (ECE)

General Eligibility Requirements

To receive graduate financial assistance, students generally must:

Be fully accepted into an eligible degree or certificate program.

Be enrolled at least part-time.

Meet satisfactory academic progress requirements if they are returning students.

Apply for financial aid according to the applicable requirements.

Financial assistance is generally not available for non-degree students, repeated courses, or courses taken for personal enrichment.

Applying for Financial Aid

Students can review their eligibility and complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) at:

https://studentaid.gov/

Eastern University's FAFSA school code is 003259.

Students should contact their Enrollment Counselor to learn about available Eastern University scholarships, assistantships, and other financial assistance.

Important Information

Federal financial aid eligibility has additional requirements, including citizenship or eligible non-citizen status. International students should contact Eastern University's Financial Aid Office to ask about funding options available to them.
